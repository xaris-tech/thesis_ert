2. CD74HC4067 Multiplexers (x4)

All 4 multiplexers share the same VCC (to 3V3), GND (to GND), and channels C0-C11 (to Electrodes E1-E12). Only the SIG/COM pin is uniquely routed for each.
Multiplexer SIG/COM Pin Connects To Purpose
MUX_I_SRC Current Pump Op-Amp (Junction at Pin 5) Routes injected current TO the tree
MUX_I_RET GND Routes return current FROM the tree
MUX_VP ADS1115 A0 Routes V+ measurement to ADC
MUX_VN ADS1115 A1 Routes V- measurement to ADC
