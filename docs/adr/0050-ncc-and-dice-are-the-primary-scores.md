# ADR-0050: NCC and Dice are the primary scores for difference images, SSIM is demoted to a diagnostic

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Affects:** how reconstruction quality is reported for difference imaging; `ssim/saline-tank-2026-10-02/README.md`. Supersedes the reporting half of [ADR-0045](0045-ssim-scoring-against-block-masks.md).
- **Related:** ADR-0045, ADR-0047, ADR-0048, ADR-0049

## Context

The question this settles: for **difference** imaging on this instrument, is it
defensible to report NCC and Dice instead of SSIM?

SSIM was the original choice (ADR-0045) and it has a specific defect here that is not a
matter of taste. SSIM's luminance term compares *absolute* brightness. The lobe this
solver produces is routinely dimmer than the template it is scored against, and SSIM
counts that dimness as a structural mismatch — it is measuring an amplitude error as
though it were a shape error. That is why SSIM needed two corrections before it produced
a usable number at all: first the whole-disc mean had to be restricted to a dilated
mask neighbourhood (an empty tank was scoring 0.904–0.957 and every run was scoring
*below* it), and then the geometry constants had to be fixed (ADR-0049).

After both fixes, on the 19-run series, each run scored against its own empty-tank
control:

| metric | min | max | mean | empty tank | beats its control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NCC** | 0.461 | 0.913 | 0.721 | 0.031 | **19 / 19** |
| **Dice** | 0.663 | 1.000 | **0.929** | 0.204 | **19 / 19** |
| SSIM | 0.060 | 0.199 | 0.155 | −0.004 | 19 / 19 |

All three now separate every run from its control. The question is therefore no longer
"which one works" but "which one to lead with", and on that the evidence is not close:
**SSIM's mean is 0.155 while Dice's is 0.929**, on identical inputs, because SSIM is
answering a question about brightness that this measurement cannot answer well.

## Decision

Report **NCC as the primary image-similarity score**, with **Dice** as the readable
secondary ("what fraction of the claimed block is where the block is"). Retain SSIM and
report it, labelled as a diagnostic for amplitude, not as the headline.

Every one of the three is reported with its empty-tank control. No score is reported on
its own.

## Rationale

- **NCC is scale-invariant, which is the property this reconstruction needs.** A correct
  but dim lobe is not penalised. It is also position-sensitive — a blob in the wrong
  place scores near zero — so it retains the one property that makes an image score mean
  anything. The rejected alternative is instructive: a registered, centroid-cropped
  variant scored 0.972 against detections *and* 0.939 against the empty tank. Removing
  position removes meaning. NCC keeps position.
- **Dice is threshold-free at the reporting level and directly interpretable.** A
  non-specialist can read "0.93 of the claimed block is where the block is". Its one
  weakness is the threshold, which is stated explicitly as `DICE_THRESHOLD` rather than
  tuned.
- **SSIM is kept, not discarded, because it sees something the others do not.** NCC is
  blind to absolute level: two lobes of very different amplitude correlate perfectly.
  SSIM catches a reconstruction whose lobe amplitude is wrong while its shape and place
  are right. That is a real defect class in EIT, where drive current and contact
  resistance set lobe amplitude.
- **SSIM's absolute range is not comparable to published EIT figures.** The literature
  reports 0.36–0.38 for NOSER and TV on simulated phantoms with absolute conductivity
  ground truth; those are absolute reconstructions against a conductivity field, a
  different quantity from a difference image against a geometric mask. Quoting 0.155
  next to 0.38 as though they were comparable would be wrong in both directions.
- **Rejected: dropping SSIM entirely.** Three metrics cost one table column each and
  have different blind spots. Reporting one and calling it *the* quality measure repeats
  the mistake ADR-0045 made.

## Consequences

- **NCC is not SSIM and must never be described as "SSIM-like", nor as SSIM.** It
  measures linear agreement of an intensity pattern. Two blobs of different size sharing
  a bright centre correlate highly. It carries no null of its own, which is why the
  control column is mandatory rather than decorative.
- **NCC and Dice against a template generated by the same solver measure reproducibility,
  not absolute localisation.** The template inherits the reconstruction's own behaviour,
  so a systematic bias in the pipeline would move both together and cancel. This is a
  property of validating a pipeline against itself, and it is why the *independent*
  evidence — angle error against photographs, median 3.4°, max 9.9° — is the localisation
  claim, and NCC is the reproducibility figure beside it.
- A reader who asks "what does an empty tank score?" gets an answer on the same line as
  every detection. That is the whole reason this ADR exists.
- `ssim_eval.py` does not emit NCC or Dice yet, so `ssim_results.csv` does not carry
  them. Deferred rather than hand-patching a generated file (ADR-0047).
