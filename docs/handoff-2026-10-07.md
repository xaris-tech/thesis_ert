## What the 2026-10-06 session did and found

Read **`docs/session-2026-10-06-absolute-disc-series.md`** (on `main`, in the zxc clone) in full. It covers:

- the code built
- the ten-disc results
- the best settings (keep the Coconut preset)
- the bench protocol
- the open questions

The decisions are in ADR-0041 to 0043 **on `main`**. Do not restate them; the key results are:

- disc-01 passed absolute gates twice: about 2.5 % reciprocity, misfit about 8 %, sigma0 2.33-2.34 mS. Its image is repeatable (r = 0.998) but its lowest region is at E10, **not** at its holes near E4-E7.
- The other nine discs were refused. **E2 was the worst or second-worst electrode on 8 of the 9**; re-clipping alone took disc-04 from 28.8 % to 8.2 %. The suspected fault is E2's lead, clip or mux channel.
- Reciprocity error does **not** correlate with current (r between -0.31 and +0.14 across 5 runs), so lowering the DAC is not a fix. Large-signal pairs, the ones next to the injecting electrodes, carry the error, which points at contacts.

## Facts the next agent must not get wrong

- **No standing coconut tree has ever been scanned.** All coconut data comes from cut discs. Run names like `coconuttree-1`, ADR-0039 and the Coconut preset's notes call these discs a "standing tree", which is wrong. A correcting ADR was proposed and is **awaiting the operator's OK**.
- **Treat disc-10's lightness as unexplained** (it could be dryness, thickness, size, a cavity or stem position). Do not call it "drier".
- **Advice to "dry the disc" means blotting the faces only**, not dehydrating the whole disc.
- **The rig is hand-built on perfboard.** Suspect bridges, flux leakage and intermittent joints first.
- **The current-sense miswiring must be checked.** The asd memory notes it as of 2026-09-02: OPA2134 pin 5 tied to pin 1. ADR-0017's "current-source repair" suggests it was fixed, but confirm against the bench before citing current values.
- **Run `20261006-171852-disc-04-intact` had swapped leads** (E1/E2 with E7/E8). Its reciprocity is valid; its misfit and image are not.
